Research Seminar · Humanizing LLM Inference Visualization

  • UX Research
  • HTML/CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Usability Testing Methods
  • Data Visualization

Academic (Germany)

Oct 2025 - Feb 2026

Our team studied how inference-time animations affect how people experience waiting for an LLM's response. I helped design 24 experimental conditions by adapting an existing web-based LLM emulator. After running individual sessions with 20 participants, I analyzed the Likert-scale ratings in Python using repeated-measures ANOVA and 95% confidence intervals, and visualized results with matplotlib. Longer waits significantly reduced satisfaction, and where the animation appeared mattered more than expected — placing it inside the chat felt clearer, but occasionally more distracting.

Usability Engineering Project · Passenger Information Display for Autonomous Buses

  • Questionnaires
  • Personas
  • Figma
  • Think-Aloud Testing

Academic (Germany)

Jun 2025 - Jul 2025

Our team designed an in-vehicle information display to help passengers understand what's happening during autonomous-bus emergencies. I ran a requirements study with a 17-question survey, synthesized the responses into four personas and built a horizontal Figma prototype covering three scenarios: ticket control, delays, and accidents. Think-aloud testing with five participants showed that simple, clear displays gave passengers a sense of orientation and trust, and pointed us toward working professionals as a group worth testing next.
